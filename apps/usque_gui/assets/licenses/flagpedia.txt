Country and region flag images

Source: Flagpedia.net
https://flagpedia.net/download/images
https://flagcdn.com/w80.zip

Flagpedia identifies these flag images as public domain and permits their
free use, including commercial and non-commercial use:
https://flagpedia.net/terms

The bundled PNG files retain their original contents, names and proportions.
Their source width is 80 pixels. Retrieval information and SHA-256 hashes
are recorded in assets/flags/manifest.json in the source repository.
