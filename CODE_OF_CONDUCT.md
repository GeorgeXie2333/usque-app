# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in the Usque community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and kindness;
- respecting different viewpoints, experiences, and technical backgrounds;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning when mistakes cause harm;
- prioritizing the health of the project and community over individual advantage.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances;
- trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, or sustained disruption;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- threats, intimidation, deliberate misrepresentation, or retaliation;
- conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, Issues, Pull Requests, and other contributions that conflict with this Code of Conduct. When appropriate, maintainers will explain moderation decisions without compromising the privacy or safety of affected people.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Usque community spaces, including Issues, Pull Requests, code review, release discussions, and official project communications. It also applies when someone publicly represents the project, uses an official project identity, or acts as an appointed project representative.

## Reporting

Do not report a conduct incident in a public Issue. Use the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/GeorgeXie2333/usque-app/security/advisories/new) and begin the title with **`[CODE OF CONDUCT]`**. The form becomes available when this repository is public and Private Vulnerability Reporting is enabled.

Include only the information needed to understand the incident. Do not expose another person's private information in attachments or logs. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy and safety to the greatest practical extent. A maintainer who is involved in a report must not make the enforcement decision alone.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers use the following community-impact levels when selecting a proportionate response.

### 1. Correction

For isolated unprofessional or unwelcome behavior, the maintainer may issue a private written correction explaining the impact and expected future behavior. A private or public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

For a significant incident or repeated behavior, the maintainer may issue a formal warning and impose a defined period of no contact or participation. Ignoring those restrictions may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

For a serious violation or sustained harmful behavior, the person may be temporarily excluded from all interaction with the project and its community. Attempts to evade the ban may result in a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

For a pattern of violations, harassment, threats, aggression, retaliation, or disparagement of groups of people, the person may be permanently excluded from public interaction with the project.
